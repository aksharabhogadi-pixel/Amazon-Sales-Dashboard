1. Project Overview

The Amazon India Sales Dashboard is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to provide a simple and easy-to-understand overview of business performance.
The main objective is to develop a management dashboard for Manoj to present to his manager, Ravi, who comes from a non-technical background.

The dashboard brings important business information together in one place through charts, graphs, tables and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). It helps management understand overall business performance without requiring technical knowledge.

The project focuses on analysing sales trends, profit performance, product performance, order status, payment methods, fulfilment status and geographic performance to support better business understanding and decision-making.

2. Dataset

The project uses an Amazon India sales dataset to analyse business and order performance.

The dataset supports analysis of:

- Sales and profit performance
- Product performance
- Order details and order status
- Customer order status
- Payment methods
- Order fulfilment
- Geographic sales performance

Dataset Details:

- Total Rows: [10000]
- Total Columns: [13]
- Date Range: [1-1-2024 to 30-8-2026]

3. Data Preparation

Data preparation was performed in Excel to make the dataset suitable for analysis and dashboard creation.

The following activities were carried out:

6. Key Findings

Based on the Amazon India Sales Dashboard analysis, the following insights were identified:

- Overall Sales and Profit Performance: The business generated total sales of ₹15,57,89,893.89 and total profit of ₹3,31,67,008.49, with an overall profit margin of 21.3%.

- Category Performance: Electronics & Mobiles is the leading category, contributing approximately ₹12.36 crore in sales and ₹2.63 crore in profit. It significantly outperforms the other product categories.

- Product Performance: Laptop Backpack is the highest-selling product among the displayed top 10 products, followed by Power Bank 20000mAh and 5G Smartphone.

- Payment Method Analysis: UPI contributes the highest profit among the displayed payment methods, indicating its importance in the business's payment          performance.

- Geographic Performance: Maharashtra records the highest state-wise sales, followed by Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, showing stronger sales performance in these      states.

- Order Performance: The order performance dashboard displays 8,147 delivered orders, 868 shipped orders, 485 returned orders and 500 cancelled orders.

- Returns and Cancellations: The displayed return percentage is 4.85%, while the cancellation percentage is 5%, highlighting opportunities to improve order fulfilment and customer experience.

7. Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following recommendations are suggested:

- Focus on High-Performing Categories: Maintain sufficient inventory and strengthen marketing strategies for Electronics & Mobiles, as it contributes the highest sales and profit.

- Promote Best-Selling Products: Focus on popular products such as Laptop Backpack, Power Bank 20000mAh and 5G Smartphone to maintain sales performance and customer demand.

- Encourage Digital Payments: Since UPI contributes the highest profit among the analysed payment methods, businesses can continue improving the UPI payment experience and encourage convenient digital transactions.

- Improve Geographic Sales Performance: Focus on Maharashtra, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, which show strong sales performance, while identifying opportunities to improve sales in lower-performing states.

- Reduce Returns and Cancellations: Analyse the reasons behind returned and cancelled orders, improve product descriptions, maintain product quality and strengthen order fulfilment processes.

- Monitor Delivery Performance: Regularly track shipped and delivered orders to identify delays and improve customer satisfaction.

8.Limitations

- Historical Data: The dashboard is based on previously available sales data and does not provide real-time business updates.

- Dataset Dependency: The accuracy of the analysis depends on the quality, completeness and reliability of the source dataset.

- Limited Analysis Period: The dashboard covers only the date range available in the dataset, so it may not represent current business performance.
